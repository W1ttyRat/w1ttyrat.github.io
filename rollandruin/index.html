Roll & Ruin

Roll the die. Survive the board.

In development for Android phones. It isn't on the Play Store yet.

How it plays

Each turn you roll a die, then choose to go, nudge the result by one, or reroll. The tile you land on decides what happens next. After ten laps the boss wakes up in the middle of the board, and every tile leads to it.

Fights play out by themselves. Your one decision is when to drink a heal potion.

Weapons, armour and trinkets come in five rarities. Rarer items carry enchants such as lifesteal, cleave, lightning, thorns and frost, and an arrow shows whether a drop is an upgrade.

Passing Start refills the board, and every fourth lap stops you in a shop. Between runs you unlock heroes, buy permanent upgrades and pick up daily missions.

How it's built

Godot 4.7 and GDScript, exported to Android. The game rules (board, combat, items, progression) live in plain classes with no scene nodes, so they run headless under 37 GUT test files.

Nothing is bought or downloaded. Every sprite, sound effect and piece of music comes out of generator scripts, and the low-poly world around the board is generated per land, the same each time you visit it.

Runs save locally and can be continued after closing the app. Signing in with Google Play Games backs progress up to the cloud.

Godot 4.7, GDScript, GUT, gdtoolkit, Google Play Games Services, AdMob, Android SDK
