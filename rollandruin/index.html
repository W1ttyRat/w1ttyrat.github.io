How it plays

Each turn you roll a die, then choose to go, nudge the result by one, or reroll. The tile you land on decides what happens next. After five laps the boss wakes up in the middle of the board, and every tile leads to it.

The boss doesn't wait quietly. Every five rolls it curses the board, and each boss has its own curse. The curses fade when you pass Start.

Fights play out by themselves. You decide when to drink a heal potion and when to use your hero's skill.

Weapons, armour and trinkets come in five rarities. Rarer items carry enchants such as lifesteal, cleave, lightning, thorns and frost, and an arrow shows whether a drop is an upgrade.

Passing Start refills the board, and on laps 2 and 4 you stop in a shop. Between runs you unlock heroes, buy permanent upgrades, pick up daily missions, play the Daily Level and open a weekly chest.

How it's built

Godot 4.7 and GDScript, exported to Android. The game rules (board, combat, items, progression) live in plain classes with no scene nodes, so they run headless under 53 GUT test files.

No asset packs are bought or downloaded. Every sprite, sound effect and piece of music comes out of generator scripts, and the low-poly world around the board is generated per land, the same each time you visit it.

Runs save locally and can be continued after closing the app. Signing in with Google Play Games backs progress up to the cloud.

The game is free. Rewarded ads only play when you ask for one, and gem packs, skins, the Valkyrie bundle and No Ads are sold through Google Play. Anonymous gameplay analytics are sent only with your consent.
