Privacy policy

Roll & Ruin for Android. Last updated 27 October 2026.

Who we are

Roll & Ruin is made by Tanel Metshein, an independent developer in Estonia, who is responsible for this game's handling of your data. Questions or requests: tanelmetshein@gmail.com.

The short version

The game has no servers of its own, no analytics and no accounts of its own. Your progress is stored on your phone. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy is also stored in your Google account. The game shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob, but only when you tap "Watch ad", and the AdMob service collects data as described below.

Data on your phone

The game saves your progress (levels, gems, upgrades, heroes, daily rewards) and your settings (sound, vibration) in its own storage on your phone. This data never leaves the phone unless you sign in with Google Play Games. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings deletes it.

Google Play Games

Signing in is optional; you can play everything as a guest. If you sign in, the game uses your Play Games player profile to show your player name and stores your progress as a Saved Game in your Google account, so it can be restored on another phone. The developer cannot see your saved data or your Google account.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete the saved data for this game, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one for a reward, such as a revive or free gems. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance data.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. Without consent you get non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Sharing and security

The developer does not sell, share or receive your personal data. Data the Google services above send is encrypted in transit.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data. Because the developer holds none, such requests are handled by the controls above or by Google. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
