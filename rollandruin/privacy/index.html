Privacy policy

Roll & Ruin for Android. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Who we are

Roll & Ruin is made by Tanel Metshein, an independent developer in Estonia, who is responsible for this game's handling of your data. Questions or requests: tanelmetshein@gmail.com.

The short version

The game has no servers or accounts of its own. Your progress is stored on your phone, and in your Google account if you sign in with Google Play Games. With your consent, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase. Rewarded ads from Google AdMob only play when you tap "Watch ad", and real-money purchases go through Google Play. We never see your name, email or payment details.

Data on your phone

The game saves your progress (levels, gems, upgrades, heroes, daily rewards, the items you bought) and your settings (sound, vibration, frame rate, analytics) in its own storage on your phone. You can erase it at any time in Settings, "Profile & Data", "Delete my data". Uninstalling the game or clearing its data in Android settings also deletes it.

Google Play Games

Signing in is optional; you can play everything as a guest, and you can sign out from the title screen. If you sign in, the game uses your Play Games player profile to show your player name and stores your progress as a Saved Game in your Google account, so it can be restored on another phone. The developer cannot see your saved data or your Google account. "Delete my data" also deletes this cloud save.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can also delete the saved data for this game, or your whole Play Games profile, in the Google Play Games settings.

Gameplay analytics

With your permission, the game sends anonymous gameplay events to Google Analytics for Firebase (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC): when a level starts and ends (level, hero, result, laps, dice rolls, which enemy won, play time), shop purchases, rewarded ads watched, tutorial steps, and days since install. They come with a random app-instance ID and basic device information (such as model, OS version and country). We do not send your name, email or Google Play Games ID, and we use the data only to balance the game and see where players stop.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is sent unless you agree to "store and/or access information on a device" in the consent form. You can switch analytics off at any time in Settings, "Anonymous analytics", or change your choice under "Ad privacy choices". The data is kept for 2 months and then deleted. "Delete my data" cannot erase events that were already sent; they expire with the rest.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one for a reward, such as a revive, free gems or a boost. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK collects data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, and crash and performance data. If you bought No Ads, the AdMob SDK is not started.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads are personalised. Without consent you get non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, "Ad privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Real-money purchases (gem packs, skins and No Ads) are processed by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The game stores which items you bought (the product ID and Google's purchase token) on your phone and in your Play Games cloud save, so your purchases come back on a new phone. If you allowed analytics, it also sends an anonymous purchase event with the product ID only. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Legal bases

Saving your progress, the cloud save and purchases are needed to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract). Analytics and personalised ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings.

Sharing and security

The developer does not sell your personal data or share it with anyone besides the Google services above, and only sees the anonymous analytics reports. Data the game and those services send is encrypted in transit.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, and withdraw your consent at any time. Most of this you can do yourself with the controls above; for anything else, email the address at the top. You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
